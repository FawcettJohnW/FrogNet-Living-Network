You turned on the light in New York City — and woke up Dan's cats. The lamp noticed the change and reported back — that's the live reading in the table above. A real bulb. A real switch. FrogNet is a private network that lets you put small, inexpensive sensors anywhere and check on them from anywhere else. No monthly bill. No tech company with access to your family's data. It just works, quietly, on its own — and keeps working even when parts of the network fail. Most "smart" devices depend on a company's cloud to function — when that company changes its pricing, gets hacked, or just decides to move on, your device stops working and your data was never really yours. FrogNet works with any device you can connect to it, from monitoring conditions in places too dangerous for people to enter, to making sure Grandma turned off the stove.
