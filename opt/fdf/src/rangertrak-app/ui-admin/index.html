Categories and groups

One tab per category the event uses (Teams, Power, Water, Ambulance, Hospitals ...). In a category, each group is the equivalent of a team, for the thing it services, with its own RAM (its chat and its members' data) when ticked; a group clients may take with no login is a default screen. Its users are below, each with a role activated when they log in; a user who leads its group signs up its own members from its device.

GroupOwn RAMNo login

A password is kept only as a verifier, made on this computer; leave it blank to keep the one they have. QR shows the user's enrollment code: they scan it with the app and type their password, and the device joins.

Behaviour

Login requiredEvery client logs in; no role is taken without a user.
Relocate when the primary is unreachableA reachable member with the app serves its RAMs on its side of a split.
Fail when the primary is unreachableThe app stops on a side that cannot see the primary.
Other RAMsRAMs that are not a team's (the general RAM is always there).

Where each RAM is served

[APP_SELECTION_V1] The app's own choice of the member that serves a RAM, from what each member's probe measured (its capability: mem_total_kb, cores, cpu_bench_total, disk_fsync_ms, mysql_running ...). Need: keys a member must have to serve it. Rank: keys compared in order, higher first; -key lower first. RAM * is every RAM not named. Nothing here: the most memory, then the most cores. A group its lead placed on its own device is served there whatever this says.

Saved assignments

The teams, users and leads live on the network's nodes. If the node holding them goes, they are gone until you put them back. Save them here after a change; Restore loads a save as an edit you review and apply, to this node or (connected through it) a fallback node. Nothing saves or restores on its own.
