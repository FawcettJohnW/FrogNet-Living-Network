Teams and roles

A role is what a client opens on. A team has its own RAM (its chat and its members' data); a role that clients may take with no login is a default screen (Team vs EOC). Others are activated at a user's login.

NameTeam (own RAM)No login

Users

Each user is assigned a role, activated when they log in. A password is kept only as a verifier, made on this computer; leave it blank to keep the one they have.
