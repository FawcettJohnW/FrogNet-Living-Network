OpenEOC · UI preview

Three apps on one set of records. Open each in its own tab: what one writes, the others show at once. Each tab is one device. Nothing leaves this browser; there is no network and no server.

🚨 EOC
The EOC function: activation, staffing, lifelines, organizations, assignments, requests, objectives, events, messages. Also the admin's authorizations, so the flow runs end to end.
👥 Field
The app an organization runs: Team, Hospital, Ambulance, Shelter, Fire, Law, Power, Water, Public Works, Communications. A lead claims a login; members join the lead.
📄 EOCAdmin
The paperwork, offline: ICS forms filled from the records, the IAP, activity logs, the after-action report, the offline package.

A run through

  1. EOC › Incident › Load the demo: authorized, no one joined. Eight logins are authorized (EOC › Authorizations shows them and their passwords' notes).
  2. Field tab: pick Team, claim alpha with password alpha1. Team Alpha shows up at the EOC, in a Teams category that did not exist before.
  3. Team Alpha › Members: set a team password. A second Field tab: Member: join your lead with a call sign and that password.
  4. Another Field tab: pick Hospital, claim cascade-general / cgh1, send a status report (Divert, 2 ED beds). The Health and Medical lifeline turns yellow.
  5. More: Ambulance medic-12 or medic-14 / medic1; Shelter island-shelter / shelter1; Power Utility cascade-pud / pud1; Communications ares / ares1. Or authorize your own in EOC › Authorizations.
  6. Try claiming alpha again from another tab: refused, the Team is served. Sign it off (Leave), and the other tab can claim it.
  7. EOC: assign Team Alpha (Assignments), take the hospital's generator request through Approved → Ordered → En route → Arrived, set objectives, fill positions.
  8. EOCAdmin: open ICS 209 or the SitRep (filled from all of the above), keep a draft, make it final, print. IAP prints the period's plan.
  9. On any screen, ✎ Note records what you think of it. EOCAdmin › Offline package lists the notes and saves everything as one file to send back.

EOC › Incident › Close the incident, or EOCAdmin › Offline package › Erase, starts over.
