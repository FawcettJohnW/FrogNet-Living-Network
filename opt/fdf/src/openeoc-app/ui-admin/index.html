OpenEOC Admin

Teams

The Command Center authorizes each Team here: its login and its password. The Team's lead, on the Command Center's LAN, claims it on a phone or a Pi with them (by the Team's QR code, or typed at Manual login), and that device hosts it; its members join through the lead. A Team joins in its app's category: the Team app's are Teams, the Hospital app's Hospitals, and so on. A Team no device holds can be claimed; one being served now cannot be taken over. A password is kept only as a verifier, made on this computer; blank keeps the one it has.

Command Center users

Who logs in to the Command Center app, by user name and password. Nobody else needs a user: a Team's members join through its lead.

Where each RAM is served

[APP_SELECTION_V1] The app's own choice of the member that serves a RAM, from what each member's probe measured (its capability: mem_total_kb, cores, cpu_bench_total, disk_fsync_ms, mysql_running ...). Need: keys a member must have to serve it. Rank: keys compared in order, higher first; -key lower first. RAM * is every RAM not named. Nothing here: the most memory, then the most cores. A group its lead placed on its own device is served there whatever this says.

Saved assignments

The Teams and Command Center users live on the network's nodes. If the node holding them goes, they are gone until you put them back. Save them here after a change; Restore loads a save as an edit you review and apply, to this node or (connected through it) a fallback node. Nothing saves or restores on its own.
